# Generated document populations

These are fictional documents generated by `packages/gen` with seed 11027, four layouts, a committed fictional vendor master and known word boxes, fields and line items. The clean partition remains 96 documents: 48 training, 24 validation and 24 test. Split membership is assigned before any degradations are generated.

Each group of twelve documents includes independently planted currency, arithmetic, unknown-vendor, purchase-order, duplicate and date defects. The currency case uses a different supported ISO currency from the vendor master's currency; the altered value is rendered and becomes extraction truth while the independent master retains its expected value. The duplicate case repeats a previous document's vendor, number and total. A business defect is different from an extraction error: recovering a deliberately inconsistent printed value correctly does not make the document eligible for approval.

The expanded test sweep uses every test parent at every configured type and level: 24 parents by six degradation types by five levels, giving 720 observations. A separate calibration population uses every validation parent with the same parameter grid, also giving 720 observations. Its parents are disjoint from test and training parents. Validation degradations are available for calibration and policy selection; test degradations remain held out for reporting. Multiple variants of one parent are correlated, so pooled uncertainty should resample parents, not treat every variant as independent.

The grids and units are committed in `sweep/config.json` and `calibration/config.json`. `level_index` always runs from zero (least degraded baseline) to four (most severe recorded level), including JPEG quality and downscale factor, whose numeric values decrease as degradation increases. Parameters were fixed before expanded test evaluation. They were not selected to obtain a particular result. Actual OCR measurements determine whether, and where, quality falls.

The six operations are rotation, Gaussian blur, independent Gaussian RGB noise, JPEG encode/decode, downscale plus nearest-neighbor restoration, and a perspective transform plus uneven lighting. A level-zero image is unchanged; JPEG uses quality 95 as its least-degraded baseline. All saved sweep images are lossless PNG containers after the specified transformation, so there is no additional undocumented JPEG save step. The `image_path` and normalized original truth tokens are retained on every observation. Geometric transforms alter the page pixels; their original boxes are reference data, not claimed to be transformed OCR detections.

No real customer, vendor or transaction is represented by these generated documents. Public CORD receipts are a separate licensed source with their own provenance.
